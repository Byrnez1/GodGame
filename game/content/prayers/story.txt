# STORY PRAYERS (the first act)
#
# Each prayer starts with "=== name".
# Prayer settings (optional, before the first blank line):
#   when:      generation it can appear: "3" (only 3), "3+" (3 or later), "3-6"
#   speaker:   who is praying
#   requires:  discoveries or flags that must exist, separated by commas
#   blocks:    discoveries or flags that stop it from appearing
#   once:      "no" if it can happen more than once (default: yes)
#   weight:    how likely it is to be picked, compared to others (default 10)
#   kind:      "villain" for Time and Memory's interference
#
# Then a blank line and the story text, then the choices.
# Each choice starts with ">" and is followed by indented settings:
#   cost:     faith it costs (the choice is greyed out if you can't afford it)
#   faith, people, love, fear:   change by this amount, like "+2" or "-3"
#   sets:     a flag to remember, used by "requires" or "blocks" elsewhere
#   grants:   give a discovery for free
#   forgets:  make the people forget a discovery
#   silent:   "yes" if this choice means ignoring the prayer
#   needs:    a rank needed to pick it: spirit, deity, or god
#   result:   what happens, shown after choosing
#
# Every prayer needs at least one choice that costs nothing.
# DRAFT TEXT: rewrite freely.

=== waking
when: 1

You wake. There is no before, only now. Far below, a few dozen people huddle together against the cold, and one of them, a young woman with ash on her cheeks, is looking straight up at you.

She cannot know you are there. And yet.

> Let her feel you watching
  faith: +2
  love: 1
  result: She smiles and does not know why. That night she tells the others that something in the sky is kind.
> Send a cold wind, so she looks away
  faith: +2
  fear: 1
  result: She pulls her furs tight and hurries back to the others. That night she tells them that something in the sky is angry.
> Do nothing
  silent: yes
  result: She watches a while longer, then turns back to the others.

=== first_offering
when: 2
speaker: Sana, who watches the sky

The young woman with ash on her cheeks has a name now, or something like one: Sana. She has started leaving small things on a flat rock for you. Berries. A bird's feather. Tonight she kneels by the rock and makes a sound that is almost a word.

> Answer with a falling star
  cost: 2
  faith: +5
  love: 1
  sets: sana_heard
  result: The star burns across the whole sky. Sana does not sleep. By morning, everyone has heard.
> Answer with thunder
  cost: 2
  faith: +5
  fear: 1
  sets: sana_heard
  result: The thunder rolls over the hills. The others cover their heads. Sana does not. She is smiling.
> Stay silent
  silent: yes
  result: Sana waits until the berries go bad. She comes back the next night anyway.

=== cold_nights
when: 1-5
blocks: fire
speaker: The people

The nights are getting longer. The old ones sleep closest to the middle of the huddle, and in the morning, not all of them wake.

> Strike a dead tree with lightning
  cost: 3
  grants: fire
  result: The tree burns for three days. They are terrified of it, and then they are not. They have fire.
> Let the cold teach them
  silent: yes
  people: -4
  result: Four of them do not wake. The rest press closer together.

=== first_words
when: 2-6
blocks: speech
speaker: Sana

Sana is trying to tell the others about you. She points at the sky, at the flat rock, at her own heart. They do not understand. She has no words for any of it.

> Give her words
  cost: 2
  grants: speech
  result: The words come to her in a dream. By the end of the season, the children are naming everything they see.
> Let her struggle
  silent: yes
  result: She gives up and points at the sky one more time. A few of them look up.

=== the_herds
when: 3-6
blocks: river, wandering
speaker: The hunters

The herds are moving south, as they do every year. Some of the hunters want to follow them. Others want to stay by the river, where the fish are easy and the reeds grow tall.

> Send a dream of the river
  cost: 2
  grants: river
  result: They stay. The children learn to fish before they learn to walk.
> Send a dream of the herds
  cost: 2
  grants: wandering
  result: They follow the herds. By the next moon, the river is far behind them.
> Let them argue
  silent: yes
  people: -3
  result: The argument turns into a fight. Three are badly hurt. Nothing is decided.

=== sana_dies
when: 3+
requires: speech
blocks: sunrise_burial, sky_burial
speaker: Sana, grey-haired now

Sana is dying. She asks how she should be laid to rest, and whether you will be there when she wakes.

> Lay her facing the sunrise
  cost: 2
  grants: sunrise_burial
  sets: sana_gone
  result: They bury her facing east, so she will always wake to something warm. You do not know why, but you could not bear any other way.
> Give her to the sky
  cost: 2
  grants: sky_burial
  sets: sana_gone
  result: They lay her on the high rocks for the birds. Her granddaughter watches the sky for you now.
> Say nothing
  silent: yes
  sets: sana_gone
  result: She dies waiting for your answer. They bury her the ordinary way, and nobody is sure what that is.

=== first_song
when: 4+
requires: speech
blocks: song
speaker: A child

A child is humming by the fire, the same few sounds over and over. The others tell her to be quiet. She keeps humming. She says the sounds are for you.

> Give the humming a melody
  cost: 4
  grants: song
  result: By morning the whole camp is singing it. They will sing it for a thousand years.
> Let the others silence her
  fear: 1
  result: She stops. Something small dies before it is born.

=== the_weaver
when: 5+
requires: river
blocks: weaving
speaker: Ama, the river-keeper

Ama has been twisting reeds together while she watches the water. Her baskets hold fish. Her mats keep the mud out of the huts. She asks you to bless her hands.

> Teach her hands the pattern
  cost: 4
  grants: weaving
  result: Within a few years every hut has mats, and every child has a basket.
> Leave her to it
  result: Her baskets are good. They might have been better.

=== the_flood
when: 5+
requires: river
speaker: Ama, the river-keeper

The river has flooded twice this season. Ama kneels in the mud and asks what the people have done wrong.

> Calm the waters
  cost: 8
  needs: spirit
  love: 1
  result: The river falls back overnight, gentle as a held breath. Ama weeps with relief.
> Send a dream of high ground
  people: -2
  sets: high_ground
  result: They move their huts up the hillside. Two are lost carrying the last of the food, but the rest are safe.
> Remain silent
  silent: yes
  people: -6
  faith: -2
  result: The river takes six of them, and the huts nearest the water. For a long time, nobody leaves gifts on the flat rock.

=== the_wall_builder
when: 6+
requires: fire
blocks: stone
speaker: The toolmakers

The toolmakers have noticed that some stones split clean when struck just so. One of them, a quiet boy, has been stacking them into walls that do not fall.

> Bless his hands
  cost: 6
  needs: spirit
  grants: stone
  result: The walls grow taller every year. Others come to learn from him.
> Let it stay a game
  result: He builds his walls. Nobody else pays attention.

=== the_rivals
when: 6+
requires: spear
speaker: The hunters

A rival band has been taking game from your people's valley. The hunters are sharpening their spears and looking to you.

> Bless the spears
  cost: 3
  fear: 2
  faith: +4
  people: -3
  result: They come back with blood on their spears and the valley to themselves. Three of them do not come back at all.
> Turn them toward peace
  cost: 3
  love: 1
  people: +4
  result: The hunters meet the rival band and share a fire instead. Some of the rivals stay.
> Stay silent
  silent: yes
  people: -2
  result: There is fighting. Nobody wins.

=== a_house_for_you
when: 8+
requires: stone, song
blocks: temple
speaker: The elders

The elders want to build you a house of stone, big enough for everyone to sing in when the storms come.

> Show them where to build it
  cost: 8
  needs: deity
  grants: temple
  result: It takes a whole generation. When it is finished, the rain on its roof sounds like a thousand small hands.
> Tell them you need no house
  result: They are disappointed, and a little relieved.

=== the_dream
when: 10+
requires: speech

Your people sleep. You do not sleep, but tonight, for the first time, something like a dream comes to you: a woman laughing, beside a river you have never seen. When it fades, you understand only one thing.

You have to find her.

> Hold on to it
  love: 1
  sets: purpose
  result: You do not know who she is. You only know that this world must become one she could be born into.
