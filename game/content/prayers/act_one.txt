# PRAYERS: ACT ONE (generations 1-5)
#
# Each prayer starts with "=== name".
# Prayer settings (optional, before the first blank line):
#   speaker:   who is praying
#   picture:   the picture shown above the prayer (see cutscenes/story.txt for the list)
#   once:      "no" if it can happen more than once (default: yes)
#   weight:    how likely it is to be picked compared to others (default 10)
#   kind:      "villain" for Time and Memory, "final" for the last choice
#   Conditions: when, requires, blocks, after, rank, mood, journal, silence
#               (see cutscenes/story.txt for what each one means)
#
# Then a blank line, the story text, and the choices.
# Each choice starts with ">" and is followed by indented settings:
#   cost:      faith it costs up front (shown to the player)
#   needs:     a rank needed to pick it: spirit, deity, or god
#   faith, people, love, fear, unrest:   change by this amount, like "+2" or "-3"
#   sets:      a flag to remember (used by conditions elsewhere)
#   grants:    give a discovery for free
#   forgets:   make the people forget a discovery
#   closes:    close a discovery's path forever
#   silent:    "yes" if this means ignoring the prayer
#   ends:      "yes" to end the game after this choice
#   result:    what happens (shown after choosing; the player never sees numbers first)
#
# CASTING THE BONES:
#   test:      "love 12", "fear 10", or "faith 14". The player casts three bones
#              (3 to 18), adds a bonus from love, fear, or rank, and can pour in
#              faith. Reaching the number means success.
#   fail_result, fail_faith, fail_people, fail_love, fail_fear, fail_unrest, fail_sets:
#              what happens instead when the cast fails
#
# Every prayer needs at least one choice that costs nothing.
# DRAFT TEXT: rewrite freely.


=== first_sight
when: 1
picture: village

Far below, a young woman with ash on her cheeks has stopped walking. She is looking straight up at you.

She cannot know you are there. And yet she does not look away.

> Let her feel warmth on her face
  love: +4
  sets: sana_met
  result: She smiles, and does not know why. That night she tells the others that something in the sky is kind. Some of them laugh at her.
> Let her feel the weight of your gaze
  fear: +4
  faith: +2
  sets: sana_met
  result: She drops to her knees in the frost. That night she tells the others that something in the sky is watching, and that it is not to be laughed at. Nobody laughs.
> Look away
  silent: yes
  sets: sana_met
  result: She watches a while longer, then turns back to the others. For a moment, you felt something like being known. Then it passed.


=== the_flat_rock
when: 2
speaker: Sana, who watches the sky

Sana has started leaving things on a flat rock for you. A feather. Berries. Tonight it is her little brother's fur blanket, and her little brother is shivering beside the fire without it.

She does not know what you want. She is trying everything.

> Accept the blanket
  faith: +4
  fear: +3
  people: -1
  sets: sana_heard
  result: The blanket is gone by morning. So, two nights later, is her brother. Sana tells the others the sky accepted her gift, and that gifts must cost something. They believe her.
> Return it to the boy, with something more
  cost: 2
  love: +5
  sets: sana_heard, sana_gift_returned
  result: In the morning the blanket is back around her brother, and a dead hare lies on the flat rock. Sana weeps. She tells the others the sky gives more than it takes.
> Leave the rock untouched
  silent: yes
  result: The blanket sits on the rock for three days. Then Sana quietly takes it back. She keeps coming to the rock anyway.


=== cold_nights
when: 1-5
blocks: fire
speaker: The people
picture: storm

The nights are getting longer. The old ones sleep in the middle of the huddle, and in the morning, not all of them wake.

> Strike a dead tree with lightning
  cost: 3
  grants: fire
  fear: +2
  result: The tree burns for three days. They are terrified of it, and then they are not. They have fire.
> Guide Sana's hands to strike sparks from stone
  test: faith 10
  grants: fire
  love: +3
  result: It takes her all night. Then a spark catches, and she is laughing, and everyone is laughing, and they have fire. They will say forever that Sana brought it, and that you guided her hand.
  fail_result: She strikes the stones until her hands bleed. Nothing catches. Two of the old ones do not wake.
  fail_people: -2
  fail_love: +1
> Let the cold teach them
  silent: yes
  people: -4
  result: Four of them do not wake. The rest press closer together, and learn something about the world, and about you.


=== naming
when: 2-6
blocks: speech
requires: sana_met
speaker: Sana

Sana is trying to tell the others about you. She points at the sky, at the flat rock, at her own chest. They do not understand. She has no words for any of it.

> Give her words in a dream
  cost: 2
  grants: speech
  result: The words come to her in a dream. By the end of the season, the children are naming everything they see. The first thing they name is you.
> Let her find them herself
  test: love 8
  grants: speech
  result: She finds them. It takes her a whole year, but she does it, and the words she makes are hers. Everyone agrees the sky helped.
  fail_result: She gives up. She points at the sky one last time, and goes back to the fire.
  fail_love: -2


=== the_herds
when: 3-6
blocks: river, wandering
speaker: The hunters
picture: village

The herds are moving south, as they do every year. Half the hunters want to follow them, as their fathers did: more meat, more furs, a people who are never caught by one bad winter. The other half want to stay by the river, where fish are easy and the reeds grow tall.

Both sides have brought you an offering. Both sides are waiting.

> Send a dream of the river
  cost: 2
  grants: river
  result: They stay. The children learn to fish before they learn to walk. Some of the hunters never forgive the river.
> Send a dream of the herds
  cost: 2
  grants: wandering
  result: They follow the herds. By the next moon the river is far behind them. They are strong, and hungry, and always moving.
> Let them settle it themselves
  silent: yes
  people: -3
  unrest: +2
  result: The argument becomes a fight. Three are badly hurt. Nothing is decided, and both sides blame you.


=== strangers
when: 3-6
speaker: The scouts
picture: village

Strangers on the ridge: a dozen people with painted faces, thin and sick-looking, watching the camp. They have children with them. They have not come closer.

Your people have barely enough for themselves.

> Bring them down to the fire
  love: +3
  people: +7
  sets: strangers_welcomed
  result: They come down from the ridge with nothing in their hands. Your people share what little there is. Within a season, the children cannot tell who was a stranger.
> Drive them away with a storm
  fear: +5
  faith: +3
  sets: strangers_driven
  result: The storm breaks over the ridge. The strangers flee into the cold. Your people say you protect them. They also say never to anger you.
> Let your people decide
  silent: yes
  result: Your people watch the strangers. The strangers watch your people. Eventually, they leave. Nobody knows where.


=== the_two_students
when: 3-6
requires: speech
speaker: Sana, grey at the temples now

Sana is getting old. She has two students, and only enough years left to teach one of them properly.

One is a girl who hums all day and makes up words to go with it. The other is a boy who knows every plant on the plain, and which ones break a fever.

Sana asks you which one to choose.

> The girl who sings
  grants: song
  love: +2
  result: Sana teaches her everything. Within a generation, the whole people sings. The boy learns what he can on his own. Some winters, it is not enough.
> The boy who knows the plants
  grants: herbs
  love: +2
  result: Sana teaches him everything. Within a generation, fevers are something people survive. The girl keeps humming, but nobody else learns her songs.
> Say nothing
  silent: yes
  result: Sana tries to teach both. She dies before she finishes with either.
  sets: sana_gone, sana_silent


=== sana_dies
when: 4+
requires: speech
blocks: sana_gone
speaker: Sana, very old now
picture: dream_river

Sana is dying. She asks you how she should be laid to rest, and whether you will be there when she wakes.

> Lay her facing the sunrise
  cost: 2
  grants: sunrise_burial
  sets: sana_gone
  result: They bury her facing east, so she will always wake to something warm. You do not know why that was the only answer you could bear.
> Give her to the sky
  grants: sky_burial
  fear: +2
  sets: sana_gone
  result: They lay her on the high rocks, for the birds and for you. Her granddaughter watches the sky now.
> Stay silent
  silent: yes
  sets: sana_gone, sana_silent
  result: She dies waiting for your answer. They bury her the ordinary way, and nobody is sure what that is anymore.


=== the_spearmaker
when: 3+
requires: fire
blocks: spear
speaker: The hunters

A hunter has found that a stick left in the fire comes out hard and sharp. The others want to know if this is a gift from you. If it is, they will use it on the herds. They might also use it on each other.

> Tell them it is
  grants: spear
  result: Every hunter carries a fire-hardened spear by the next season. The hunting is better. The arguments are worse.
> Tell them it is not
  love: +1
  result: They put the sharpened stick aside, a little afraid of it. It will be found again. These things always are.
