# These can come up more than once, to fill quiet generations.

=== hungry_winter
when: 3+
once: no
weight: 4
speaker: The people

The stores are thin and the winter is long. The children are hungry.

> Lead the deer to the hunters
  cost: 3
  people: +2
  love: 1
  result: The deer walk into the hunters' camp as if they were called. Nobody goes hungry.
> Demand an offering first
  faith: +3
  fear: 1
  people: -1
  result: They burn the last of the dried fish for you. You send the deer. One child does not survive the wait.
> Stay silent
  silent: yes
  people: -2
  result: They get through it. Most of them.

=== good_year
when: 2+
once: no
weight: 3
speaker: The people

A good year. The children are fat, the hunting is easy, and the people leave flowers on the flat rock for you.

> Bless the year
  cost: 2
  faith: +4
  love: 1
  result: They will tell stories about this year for a long time.
> Accept their gifts
  faith: +2
  result: You take what they give.

=== the_sickness
when: 4+
once: no
weight: 3
speaker: The healers

A coughing sickness is moving from hut to hut.

> Burn it out of them with fever
  cost: 3
  fear: 1
  people: -1
  result: The fever is terrible, but it breaks the sickness. Only one dies.
> Heal them gently
  cost: 6
  needs: spirit
  love: 1
  result: The sickness simply leaves, as if it had somewhere else to be.
> Remain silent
  silent: yes
  people: -5
  result: Five are buried before the coughing stops.

=== strangers
when: 4-9
speaker: The scouts

Strangers have been seen on the ridge: a handful of people with painted faces, watching the camp. They have not come closer.

> Make them welcome
  cost: 3
  people: +6
  love: 1
  result: The strangers come down from the ridge with gifts. Within a generation, nobody remembers who was a stranger.
> Drive them away with a storm
  cost: 3
  faith: +3
  fear: 2
  result: The storm breaks over the ridge and the strangers flee. Your people say you protect them. They also say not to anger you.
> Let your people decide
  silent: yes
  result: Your people watch the strangers. The strangers watch your people. Eventually, they leave.

=== the_spearmaker
when: 3+
requires: fire
blocks: spear
speaker: The hunters

One of the hunters has found that a stick held in the fire comes out hard and sharp. The others want to know if this is a gift from you.

> Tell them it is
  cost: 3
  grants: spear
  result: Every hunter carries a fire-hardened spear by the next season.
> Let them work it out themselves
  result: Some of them do. Most of them don't bother.
