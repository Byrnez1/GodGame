# EVERYDAY PRAYERS AND UNREST
# These can come up more than once, to fill quiet generations.
# Prayers with "mood: restless" jump to the front when your people are restless.
# The same goes for the danger zones at the bottom: terrified, fearless, forsaken.
# See act_one.txt for every setting you can use.
# DRAFT TEXT: rewrite freely.


=== lean_season
when: 3+
once: no
weight: 4
speaker: The people

A lean season. Not a disaster, only hunger, the ordinary kind. They ask for nothing in particular. They just want to know you are there.

> Send a good hunt
  cost: 3
  love: +3
  people: +2
  result: The herds come close. The people eat, and sing about it.
> Ask for a share of what little they have
  fear: +3
  faith: +3
  result: They burn a share of their thin stores for you. They are hungrier, and more certain you are real.
> Drive the fish into the weirs
  needs: weirs
  people: +2
  love: +1
  result: The weirs fill so fast the children can scoop fish out with their hands. Nobody goes hungry.
> Lead the herds close to camp
  needs: herding
  people: +2
  love: +1
  result: The tame herds wander in close, fat and slow. Nobody goes hungry.
> Let it pass
  silent: yes
  result: The lean season passes, as lean seasons do.


=== a_wedding
when: 4+
once: no
weight: 3
requires: speech
speaker: Two families

Two families want to join their children in marriage, and ask your blessing. But the girl has asked, quietly, at the flat rock, not to be given to this boy. She loves someone else.

> Bless the marriage the families want
  fear: +2
  faith: +2
  result: The families are joined. The girl does as she is told. The people learn that you value order.
> Bless the one she chose
  love: +3
  unrest: +1
  result: The girl marries the one she loves. The families are furious, for a while. The people learn that you listen.


=== the_doubter
when: 5+
weight: 6
mood: restless
speaker: The elders

A man named Kesh is telling everyone that there is nothing in the sky. That the flat rock is just a rock. That you are a story the old ones tell to keep the young ones in line. People are listening.

> Strike him down
  fear: +6
  unrest: -4
  people: -1
  result: Lightning, out of a clear sky. Nobody listens to that kind of talk again. Not out loud.
> Show him a sign
  test: faith 12
  love: +3
  unrest: -3
  result: Kesh sees something on the high rocks one night that he will never describe. He becomes your most faithful priest.
  fail_result: Nothing happens. Kesh laughs, and more people laugh with him.
  fail_unrest: +3
  fail_love: -2
> Let him talk
  silent: yes
  unrest: +2
  result: He talks. Some people listen. Some stop leaving gifts on the flat rock.


=== the_revolt
when: 6+
once: no
weight: 6
mood: restless
speaker: The priests
picture: storm

The people have turned on your priests. They say the offerings are too heavy, the punishments too cruel, the sky too silent. They have torn down the flat rock.

> Crush it
  fear: +6
  people: -5
  unrest: -6
  result: You crush it. The people put the rock back up, stone by stone, without being asked. They will not tear it down again.
> Give them what they want
  fear: -8
  love: +3
  unrest: -5
  faith: -3
  result: The offerings are lightened. The punishments end. The people put the rock back up themselves, and they are not afraid of it anymore. Some of them are not much interested in it either.
> Let them rage
  silent: yes
  unrest: -2
  people: -6
  result: Some of them leave in the night, and do not come back. Six of them. The rest go quiet, for now.


=== the_flight
when: 5+
weight: 4
mood: restless
speaker: The scouts

A whole family has packed their furs and left in the night, heading for the hills. Others are talking about following them. They say they'd rather take their chances without a god than with you.

> Bring them back, by force
  fear: +4
  unrest: -2
  result: They come back. They are not happy about it.
> Let them go with your blessing
  love: +2
  people: -5
  unrest: -3
  result: Five of them walk away into the hills, and you let them. The ones who stay are the ones who want to.


=== the_fever
when: 5+
weight: 5
speaker: The healers

A fever has come with the warm rains. It is the kind that takes the very young and the very old. The healers have tried everything they know.

> Brew the fever-leaf
  needs: herbs
  love: +1
  result: The bitter tea goes round every hut. A few nights of sweating, and the fever breaks. Nobody is buried.
> Burn the huts of the sick
  fear: +4
  people: -3
  result: The fever stops spreading. Three families sleep under the sky for the rest of the season, and three of their old ones never come in from it.
> Sit with them, and hope
  test: love 10
  love: +2
  people: -1
  result: The fever burns itself out. Only one is lost.
  fail_result: The fever takes its time, and takes six.
  fail_people: -6

=== the_climb
when: 5+
weight: 6
mood: fearless
speaker: The elders
picture: storm

Some of the young men have decided to climb the high rocks, all the way to the top, to see if anything is really up there.

The elders don't stop them. The elders want to know too.

> Strike the rocks with lightning
  fear: +8
  people: -2
  result: The lightning finds the two at the top. The others climb down without being told. Nobody goes up the high rocks again for a hundred years.
> Show them something they cannot explain
  cost: 3
  fear: +4
  love: +1
  result: At the top, they find a light with no source, warm on their faces. They come down very quiet, and do not climb again.
> Let them climb
  silent: yes
  faith: -2
  unrest: +2
  result: They reach the top and find nothing but wind. They come down and tell everyone. Some people stop leaving gifts on the flat rock.


=== the_man_who_would_be_god
when: 6+
weight: 5
mood: fearless
speaker: The priests

A man named Haro has started taking the offerings from the flat rock for himself. He says the sky never touches them anyway, so why shouldn't he? He says that if there is a god, it is him: he is the one who feeds them, judges them, keeps them safe.

People are listening.

> Humble him before everyone
  test: fear 9
  fear: +6
  unrest: -2
  result: Haro wakes up unable to speak. He lives a long life, and never says another word. Nobody takes from the flat rock again.
  fail_result: Nothing happens to Haro. He laughs, and takes the next offering too.
  fail_unrest: +3
  fail_faith: -3
> Let the people judge him
  love: +2
  unrest: +1
  result: The people argue for three days. In the end, they send Haro away, but not because of you. Because he was a thief. You are not sure that's better.
> Let him take what he likes
  silent: yes
  faith: -4
  result: Haro grows fat on the offerings. Some people start bringing their gifts to Haro instead of the rock. It's quicker.


=== the_hidden_children
when: 4+
once: no
weight: 6
mood: terrified
speaker: The mothers
picture: storm

The mothers have begun hiding their newborns from the sky. They keep them wrapped in the dark of the huts, and paint their faces with mud, so you will not notice them and take them.

> Let them hide
  fear: -4
  love: +1
  result: You look away from the huts. After a while, the mothers stop painting the babies' faces. It is not trust. But it is less fear.
> Show them you see everything
  fear: +5
  faith: +3
  people: -2
  result: A storm lifts the roof off a hut, and a mother finds her child gone in the morning. Nobody hides anything from you again.
> Send a sign of gentleness
  cost: 3
  fear: -8
  love: +4
  result: Every newborn wakes the next morning with a little white flower on its blanket. The mothers do not know what to make of it. Some of them cry.


=== the_unasked_offering
when: 6+
weight: 5
mood: terrified
speaker: The priests
picture: fire

They have started giving you things you never asked for. This morning, they brought a child to the high rocks, and they are waiting for you to take her.

> Take what is offered
  fear: +6
  faith: +6
  people: -1
  sets: child_offered
  result: You take her. The people are terrified and relieved. You feel larger, and you hate it.
> Refuse, terribly
  fear: +2
  love: +1
  result: Lightning scatters the priests from the rocks. The child runs home. The priests understand that you did not want her. They do not understand why.
> Refuse, gently
  cost: 4
  fear: -10
  love: +5
  result: When the priests turn back to the rock, the child is asleep, and a dead stag lies beside her: a better gift than they gave you. The people begin, slowly, to breathe again.


=== the_other_altar
when: 4+
weight: 6
mood: forsaken
speaker: The elders

Your people have built a second altar on the hill, to a spirit of the hill. They say that at least the hill is always there.

> Tear it down
  fear: +6
  love: -2
  result: You split the altar with lightning. They come back to the flat rock. They do not come back happy.
> Win them back
  cost: 4
  love: +6
  result: The hill spirit's altar is left alone. But for a whole season, every prayer said at your flat rock is answered with something small and kind. By the end of it, the hill altar is overgrown.
> Let them pray where they like
  silent: yes
  faith: -3
  sets: other_altar
  result: Half your people pray to the hill now. The hill does not answer them either, but they don't seem to mind.


=== the_empty_rock
when: 4+
once: no
weight: 4
mood: forsaken
speaker: The flat rock

Nobody has left anything on the flat rock for a whole season. Moss is growing on it.

> Leave a gift for them, unasked
  cost: 3
  love: +5
  result: In the morning there is honey on the flat rock, and nobody knows where it came from. The children find it first. They keep coming back to check.
> Demand an offering
  fear: +4
  faith: +2
  result: A cold wind blows through the camp for a week, until someone thinks to leave something on the rock. Then it stops.
> Wait
  silent: yes
  result: The moss spreads.
