=== the_fog
when: 7+
requires: song
kind: villain

A fog rolls in from nowhere. It sits on the camp for three days. When it lifts, the people gather to sing and find they cannot remember how the song goes.

> Send the song back to them in dreams
  cost: 6
  result: They wake humming. It takes a season, but the song comes back, almost the same as before.
> Let the song go
  forgets: song
  result: The song is gone. On the journal's pages, words you had only just learned to read go dark again.

=== the_lost_generation
when: 9+
kind: villain

You blink. When you look down again, a whole generation has passed without you. Children you watched being born are old. The people say you abandoned them.

> Reach for them
  faith: -4
  love: -1
  result: They do not hear you at first. Then, slowly, some of them do.
