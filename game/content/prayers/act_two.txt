=== the_flood
when: 5+
requires: river
speaker: Ama, the river-keeper
picture: storm

The river is rising faster than it ever has. By nightfall it will take the low ground.

On the low ground: the huts where the children sleep. On the high ground: the winter stores, the dried fish, everything that will keep them alive until spring. There is only time to carry one of them to safety.

Ama asks which.

> Save the children
  love: +4
  sets: famine_coming
  result: They carry the children up the hill through the black water. All of them live. In the morning, the stores are gone.
> Save the stores
  fear: +3
  people: -6
  sets: flood_children
  result: They haul the stores to safety while the water takes the low huts. Six children. Ama's son is one of them. The people will eat this winter. They will not forget what it cost.
> Hold back the river
  test: faith 13
  love: +5
  result: The water stops at the edge of the low ground, as if it hit a wall. It stands there all night, trembling. In the morning, it goes home. Ama does not stop shaking for a week.
  fail_result: You push against the river with everything you are, and it pushes back. It takes the low huts and the stores. Four children. Half the fish.
  fail_people: -4
  fail_sets: famine_coming


=== hungry_winter
after: famine_coming 1
blocks: winter_passed
speaker: The elders
picture: storm

The winter after the flood. There is not enough food for everyone. The elders have decided: either the hunters eat first, so they stay strong enough to hunt, or the children eat first, and the hunters go out weak.

> The hunters eat first
  fear: +2
  people: -4
  sets: winter_passed
  result: The hunters stay strong. They bring back enough, in the end. Four of the smallest children do not see the spring.
> The children eat first
  love: +2
  test: love 10
  sets: winter_passed
  result: The hunters go out half-starved and come back with a bull elk. Everyone eats. They say you walked beside them.
  fail_result: The hunters go out weak. Two do not come back. The children live, and grow up without fathers.
  fail_people: -2
  fail_love: +1
> Feed them all yourself
  cost: 6
  love: +3
  sets: winter_passed
  result: Fish leap from the frozen river onto the ice. Nobody can explain it. Nobody starves.


=== reeds_or_fish
when: 4+
requires: river
blocks: weaving
speaker: Ama, the river-keeper

The river can't give both this year. Either the reed beds are left to grow tall, and Ama's people learn to weave mats and baskets and boats, or the reeds are cut back so the fish can spawn, and nobody goes hungry.

> Let the reeds grow
  grants: weaving
  people: -2
  result: The reeds grow taller than a man. Ama's hands are never still again. It is a lean year, and two of the old ones don't see the end of it, but every hut has a mat by the time the snow comes.
> Cut them back for the fish
  people: +4
  love: +2
  result: The river is thick with fish. Everyone eats well. Ama watches the cut reeds float away and says nothing.


=== the_wall_builder
when: 6+
requires: fire
blocks: stone
speaker: Teo, a quiet boy

Teo has found that certain stones split clean when struck just so. He has been stacking them into walls that do not fall. He asks you what he should build.

The elders want a wall around the camp, to keep out raiders. Teo's mother wants a house big enough for everyone to sing in when the storms come.

> A wall, to keep them safe
  grants: stone
  fear: +2
  sets: walls_built
  result: The wall grows a little every year. Behind it, your people sleep easier. They also start to feel that everything outside it is an enemy.
> A house, for singing
  grants: stone
  love: +2
  sets: song_house
  result: The house goes up slowly, stone by stone. It is not finished in Teo's lifetime. But on stormy nights, people go there, and sing.
> Let it stay a game
  result: Teo builds his little walls. The children knock them down. Nobody else pays attention.

=== drought
when: 5-11
speaker: The elders
picture: village

No rain since spring. The river is a trickle, the plain is dust, and the elders have made a decision without asking you.

They will give you the best of them: a young hunter named Oren, strong, kind, loved by everyone. They will give him to you on the high rocks, and you will send rain.

He has agreed. They are waiting for your sign.

> Accept Oren
  fear: +8
  faith: +6
  people: -1
  sets: oren_given
  result: Oren climbs the high rocks. In the morning there are clouds. By evening, rain. Your people dance in it. Oren's mother does not.
> Refuse him, and send rain anyway
  test: love 12
  love: +6
  sets: oren_spared
  result: Lightning splits the sky before they can raise the knife. Then rain, hard and warm. Oren comes down from the rocks laughing. Your people will tell this story for a thousand years: the god who would not take a life.
  fail_result: You stop the knife. You cannot bring the rain. The drought drags on, and they wonder whether refusing the gift was the mistake.
  fail_people: -5
  fail_unrest: +3
  fail_sets: oren_spared
> Refuse him, and let the drought run its course
  love: +2
  people: -6
  unrest: +2
  sets: oren_spared
  result: You stop the knife. The rain comes when it comes, a month too late. Six die of thirst. Oren lives. Some of them hate him for it.


=== sickness
after: strangers_welcomed 2
speaker: The healers

A coughing sickness, the kind the strangers had, is moving through the camp. The healers want to know: do they shut the sick out in the cold, so it spreads no further, or do they nurse them by the fire, and risk everyone?

> Shut the sick out
  fear: +3
  people: -4
  result: The coughing stops. So do the people outside. Four of them. Their families sit at the edge of the firelight all winter, looking out.
> Nurse them by the fire
  test: love 11
  love: +4
  people: -1
  result: The healers sit up with the sick night after night. The sickness burns through and leaves. Only one dies.
  fail_result: The sickness takes the camp. Nine are buried before it lets go.
  fail_people: -9
  fail_love: +1

=== the_prophecy
after: purpose 1
speaker: Ina, the priestess, Sana's great-granddaughter
picture: fire

Ina says you have been speaking to her in dreams. She is not entirely wrong: something of your longing leaks into the minds of the faithful. She has dreamed of a river, and a woman.

She has decided what it means, and is about to tell everyone.

> "The god wants a sacrifice"
  fear: +6
  faith: +6
  sets: prophecy_sacrifice
  result: Ina announces that a woman from the river will be given to you, and through her, you will become the greatest of gods. The people are frightened, and thrilled.
> "The god is searching for someone he lost"
  love: +4
  sets: prophecy_lost
  result: Ina announces that you are searching for someone, and that the people must help you find her. They don't know what that means. They begin looking at every girl born by the river a little differently.
> Silence her
  fear: +2
  unrest: +2
  result: Ina wakes with no memory of the dream. She is confused and frightened. So are the people she had already started telling.


=== the_girl_on_the_rocks
after: prophecy_sacrifice 2
speaker: Ina, the priestess
picture: fog

Ina has found a girl by the river, born facing the sunrise, who hums songs nobody taught her. Ina is certain: this is the one. She has the girl on the high rocks, and the knife in her hand.

"Make us great," she says.

> Stop her
  test: fear 12
  fear: +3
  result: The knife flies from Ina's hand as if struck. The girl runs. Ina falls on her face before you, and does not get up for a long time.
  fail_result: The knife falls. You were not strong enough to stop it. Ina raises her bloody hands to the sky and calls it a gift.
  fail_fear: +6
  fail_faith: +8
  fail_people: -1
  fail_sets: blood_on_the_river
> Stop her, gently
  cost: 5
  love: +3
  result: The girl simply isn't there when Ina turns back. She is down by the river, unharmed, humming.
> Let it happen
  fear: +10
  faith: +12
  people: -1
  sets: blood_on_the_river
  result: Ina gives you the girl. Power floods into you, more than you have ever felt. Somewhere in the old book, a page goes very still.
