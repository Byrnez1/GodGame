=== the_hymn
after: journal_is_mine 1
speaker: Ina's daughter
picture: fire

Ina's daughter has written a hymn about you. Everyone sings it. In it, you are vast and terrible and kind, and you have always watched over them, and you always will.

You know now how much of that love was put into them by someone else.

> Let them sing it
  love: +4
  faith: +3
  result: They sing it at every fire. It is beautiful, and it is not entirely theirs.
> Teach them a truer verse
  test: love 12
  love: +2
  sets: truer_verse
  result: A new verse appears, as if it was always there. It says you are a god who forgets, and is sorry. They sing it more softly than the others.
  fail_result: The new verse comes out wrong. It frightens them. They stop singing for a season.
  fail_love: -4
  fail_unrest: +2


=== the_girl_grows
after: her_born 1
speaker: Her mother
picture: birth

The girl born by the river is five years old. She hums songs no one taught her. She talks to the sky as if it might answer. Her mother is frightened and asks you to make her ordinary.

> Leave her as she is
  love: +3
  result: She keeps humming. Some days she looks up, and you could swear she sees you.
> Make her ordinary, so she is safe
  love: -2
  sets: girl_hidden
  result: She stops humming. She stops looking up. Her mother is grateful. You feel something close, like a door.


# ------------------------------------------------------------ THE FINAL CHOICE

=== final_with_her
when: 15
requires: her_born
kind: final
picture: voices

She is grown now. She stands by the river at dusk and looks up, the way Sana once did, the way she always does.

To go to her, you would have to leave the Pantheon, and in your world, the Voices can touch you.

"Go to her," says Time, "and it happens as it always has. You will love her. We will gather it. You will forget. She will be born again, and so will you."

"Or refuse us," says Memory, "and no one knows what happens. No one has ever refused."

> Go to her, and stand against them with your people's love
  test: love 15
  sets: broke_cycle, broke_with_love
  ends: yes
  result: Your people's love rises up through you like a tide, and for the first time, it is yours. The Voices cannot hold you. You walk out of the Pantheon and down to the river, and you do not come back.
  fail_result: The love in you is not enough, or not yours. The Voices close around you, gently, the way they always have.
  fail_sets: kept_cycle
> Go to her, and stand against them with your people's fear
  test: fear 15
  sets: broke_cycle, broke_with_fear
  ends: yes
  result: Your people's terror roars up through you, and the Voices flinch. You tear free of them. You go down to the river as something they have never seen: a god who cannot be harvested.
  fail_result: Fear is not enough to stand against Time. The Voices close around you.
  fail_sets: kept_cycle
> Go to her, and let it all happen again
  sets: kept_cycle
  ends: yes
  result: You go to her. You love her. It is everything the old book said it was. And then, as it always does, the light comes.


=== final_without_her
when: 15
requires: no_birth
kind: final
picture: voices

"She will not be born here," says Memory. "This world is spent. We will begin again."

"You could refuse us," says Time, almost kindly. "But for what? She is not here."

> Let them begin again
  sets: kept_cycle
  ends: yes
  result: You close your eyes. You will wake in a hall of light with no memory of anything before, and somewhere, a book will be waiting.
> Refuse them anyway
  test: faith 16
  sets: broke_cycle, broke_alone
  ends: yes
  result: You refuse. For the first time in longer than you can remember, you are a god with nothing taken from him, and no one to love. You stay with your people.
  fail_result: You refuse, and it changes nothing. The light comes.
  fail_sets: kept_cycle


=== final_fallback
when: 15
blocks: her_born, no_birth
kind: final
picture: pantheon

Time runs out before you understand what any of it meant. The Voices are waiting.

> Close your eyes
  sets: kept_cycle
  ends: yes
  result: You will wake in a hall of light with no memory of anything before.
