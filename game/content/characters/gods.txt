=== you
name: You
group: gods
portrait: you
title: a god, apparently

> You woke in a hall of light with no memory of anything before.
> Two Voices told you these people are yours to watch over. They did not tell you why.
  requires: voices_met
> No god may touch another inside the Pantheon. It is the oldest rule.
  requires: rule_learned
> You found a book in a room you don't remember, in a hand you can't read.
  requires: journal_found
> When your people fear you, you feel larger. When they love you, you feel known.
  when: 4+
> You lied to the quiet Voice, and did not know why.
  requires: lied_to_them
> Another god welcomed you "back." You do not remember leaving.
  requires: welcome_back
> You dreamed, though gods do not dream. A river. A woman laughing.
  requires: dreamed_of_her
> You are looking for her. You don't know why.
  requires: purpose
> You took a gift from the tall Voice. You cannot remember what it cost.
  requires: took_the_gift
> The old book is in your handwriting. You wrote it, and you forgot.
  requires: journal_is_mine
> You have done all of this before. Many times.
  requires: harvest_known
