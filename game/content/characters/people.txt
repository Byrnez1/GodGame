# PEOPLE (the "Known" tab)
#
# The god's own notes about the people in his world.
# They live in the Known tab, grouped by "group": people or places.
# (Gods live in gods.txt and show in the Hall, in the Pantheon view.)
#
# Each person starts with "=== name".
# Settings:
#   name:    shown as the heading
#   title:   a short description next to the name
#   group:   people, places, or gods (default: people)
#   renamed: a new name once a flag is true (see gods.txt)
#   Conditions decide when the person first appears in your notes:
#            when, requires, blocks, after, rank, mood, journal, silence
#
# Then optional intro text, then notes. Each note starts with ">" and can have
# indented conditions saying when the god learns it. Once learned, a note stays.
# DRAFT TEXT: rewrite freely.


=== sana
name: Sana
title: the first to look up
requires: sana_met

> A young woman with ash on her cheeks. She looked up at you before anyone else did.
> She leaves gifts for you on a flat rock.
  requires: sana_heard
> You gave her brother back his blanket, and something more. She has not forgotten.
  requires: sana_gift_returned
> She taught her people to speak, and the first thing they named was you.
  requires: speech
> She is buried facing the sunrise.
  requires: sunrise_burial, sana_gone
> She was given to the sky.
  requires: sky_burial, sana_gone
> She died waiting for your answer.
  requires: sana_silent


=== reed_woman
name: The woman in the reeds
title: a story your people tell
requires: reed_woman_seen

> Your people say she is a spirit to be feared.
  requires: reed_woman_feared
> Your people say she is a good omen. They leave river stones for her.
  requires: reed_woman_omen
> The children have a game about her. One is the woman. The others must not look at her.
  when: 5+
> Nobody remembers her anymore.
  requires: reed_story_lost
> The children play her game again, as if they made it up.
  requires: reed_story_kept
> The woman in your dream was standing in the reeds.
  requires: dreamed_of_her
> The girl by the river stands at dusk exactly where the reed-cutters once saw her.
  requires: her_born
