# ENDINGS AND EPILOGUES
#
# ENDINGS: checked from top to bottom when the game ends. The first one whose
# conditions are all true is shown. Keep one with no conditions at the bottom.
# EPILOGUES ("kind: epilogue"): every one whose conditions are true is added
# after the ending, so the ending reflects how the player ruled.
#
# Settings:
#   title:   the heading
#   kind:    ending (default) or epilogue
#   Conditions: requires, blocks, mood (loved / feared / restless / calm),
#               journal ("70+" = 70% of her world rebuilt), silence ("5+" = five or
#               more prayers ignored), people ("none" = everyone died)
# DRAFT TEXT: rewrite freely.


=== silence
people: none
title: Silence

There is no one left to pray to you. The flat rock where Sana once left berries is overgrown. Somewhere, a book you wrote will never be finished.


=== broke_with_love
requires: broke_with_love
title: The first spring

You walk out of the Pantheon and down to the river, and you do not go back.

The harvest ends. Across every world, a little of the love that people felt for their gods quietly drains away, and people look up at the sky and feel, for the first time, nothing in particular. Some gods fade. Some temples empty.

But by the river, a woman you have loved more times than you can count turns and sees you, and this time, you will remember.


=== broke_with_fear
requires: broke_with_fear
title: A god who cannot be harvested

You tear free of the Voices and go down to her as something new: a god held together by terror, and too strong to be taken apart.

The harvest ends. The other gods fade a little, in every world. You do not. Your people fear you enough to keep you strong forever.

She looks at you by the river. She is not sure, yet, whether she should be afraid. Neither are you.


=== broke_alone
requires: broke_alone
title: A god with nothing taken

You refuse them, and for once, nothing is taken. You have no one to love, and you remember all of it.

You stay with your people. You answer their prayers. You do not know what happens to a world without the harvest. You are going to find out.


=== kept_cycle
requires: kept_cycle
title: Again

Light. Then more light.

You are somewhere. You are someone. Beyond that, nothing.

Somewhere nearby, in a room you have never seen, there is a book. It is a little thicker than it was.


=== the_age_ends
title: The age ends

The age ends before you understand what it meant.
