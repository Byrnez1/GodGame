# DISCOVERIES (the decision web, shown as stars in the Pantheon)
#
# Each one starts with "=== name". The name is how other files refer to it.
#
# Settings (all optional):
#   name:      what the player sees
#   rank:      whisper, spirit, deity, or god (the rank you need to grant it)
#   cost:      faith to grant it
#   requires:  other discoveries needed first, separated by commas
#   excludes:  discoveries this one closes off forever
#   income:    extra faith every generation
#   growth:    extra % population growth every generation
#   love:      love gained when granted
#   fear:      fear gained when granted
#   sight:     lets you see further through the fog in the heavens
#   glimpse:   a hint about the future, shown once you're a Deity
#
# WHEN IT APPEARS (the fog of war):
#   A star is hidden in fog until the story reveals it. Until then, the player
#   only sees a faint "?" if it's next to something they already have.
#   appears:   flags or discoveries; the star appears once ANY of them is true.
#              Leave it out and the star appears as soon as its 'requires' are met.
#   from:      the earliest generation it can appear
#   until:     the last generation it can be granted. After that, its moment
#              has passed and it's lost forever.
#   lost:      what the player is told when its moment passes
#
# Then a blank line, then the description.
# DRAFT TEXT: rewrite freely.
