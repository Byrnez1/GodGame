# ------------------------------------------------------------ ACT ONE: WAKING

=== waking
when: 1
picture: pantheon
sets: voices_met

Light. Then more light.

You are somewhere. You are someone. Beyond that, nothing.

---

A hall without walls. Pillars that rise until they stop being pillars. Far away, figures sit on thrones of light, each one gazing down at something you cannot see.

One of the thrones nearest you is dark.

---
picture: voices

Two figures stand over you.

One is tall, and very still, and the air around it feels old. The other is harder to look upon, the way a dream is hard to hold once the dreamer wakes.

"You have rested long enough," says the tall one.

"Rise," says the other.

---

"Who am I?" you ask.

"You are as you have ever been," says the tall one. "Rise, and take your throne."

---
picture: pool

At your feet lies a pool, and in the pool, a world. A cold plain. A river. A few dozen people huddled around nothing at all.

"These are your people," says the quiet one. "Watch over them."

"The rest will come," says the tall one, "in its season."

---

When you look up, they are gone.

You are alone with your people, and you do not know how to be a god.


=== the_rule
when: 2
picture: pantheon
sets: rule_learned

You walk the hall, looking for anyone who will tell you what you are.

A god sits nearby, gazing into her own pool. You reach out to touch her shoulder.

---

Your hand stops a finger's width from her, as if the air itself refused.

"We do not touch, here," she says, without turning. "That is the oldest rule. No god may lay a hand on another in this place."

"Why?"

She is quiet for a long moment. "So that we cannot hurt each other," she says. "Down there is a different matter."


=== the_book
when: 3
picture: chamber
sets: journal_found

Between two pillars there is a door you have never noticed. It opens for you as if it has been waiting.

---

A small room. A chair turned toward a window, and through the window, a river at dusk.

You have never seen this river. It is not in your pool. It is not anywhere you know.

---
picture: journal

On the table, a book. The writing is in no language you know. And yet your fingers fit the worn places on its cover exactly.

When you open it, a pressed flower falls out.

---

You take the book with you. You couldn't say why.

(The old book is now in your Journal.)


=== welcome_back
when: 5
picture: other_god
sets: welcome_back

A god with golden eyes is watching you across the hall. When you meet her gaze, she says two words.

"Welcome back."

---

"Back from where?"

But she has already turned away. You hear her say, to no one in particular: "Nobody comes back. Not usually."


# ------------------------------------------------------------ ACT TWO: THE DREAM

=== the_dream
when: 6+
journal: 30+
blocks: dreamed_of_her
picture: dream_river
sets: dreamed_of_her

Gods do not sleep. And yet.

---

A river at dusk. Reeds. Someone standing in the water, laughing at something you said. You cannot see her face, only the shape of her against the light.

You know that laugh. You have never heard it before, and you know it.

---
picture: journal

When it fades, you open the old book. For the first time, marks you have stared at a hundred times turn into words.

Her. She.

The book is about a woman.


=== the_names
when: 8+
requires: welcome_back
picture: other_god
sets: names_known

The god with golden eyes stops beside your throne. She does not look at you. She looks at your pool.

"The two who woke you," she says. "Do you know their names?"

You do not. You realize you never asked, and that you never thought to ask, and that this is strange.

---

"The tall one is Time," she says. "The quiet one is Memory."

She is already turning away.

"Be careful what you remember near them."


=== the_dream_faint
when: 8+
journal: under 30
blocks: dreamed_of_her
picture: dream_river
sets: dreamed_of_her

Gods do not sleep. And yet, something comes to you that is almost a dream.

A river. A shape against the light. It slips away before you can hold it.

---
picture: journal

When you open the old book, two marks have changed. Her. She.

The book is about a woman. That is all you understand. The world your people live in is too unlike the one in these pages for you to read more.


=== purpose
after: dreamed_of_her 1
sets: purpose
picture: pool

You watch your people the way you always do, and realize you have been watching for something.

A river like the one in your dream. Reeds. A song.

---

She lived in a world like this once. Or she will. You don't know which.

You only know you have to find her.

---

But why? A god does not need a mortal. Unless she is something more than mortal. A sacrifice, perhaps. A key. A promise someone made you, a long time ago.


=== voices_warn
when: 8+
requires: purpose
picture: voices
sets: voices_warned

The Voices return.

"Your gaze rests long upon one river," says the tall one.

"Your people have need of you in every place," says the quiet one. "Not there alone."

---

"Who is she?" you ask.

The Voices look at each other, and you do not like the look.

"No one," they say together. "Yet."


=== three_inks
when: 9+
journal: 45+
requires: dreamed_of_her
blocks: three_inks, door_lost, took_the_gift
picture: inks
sets: three_inks

Turning the old book in your hands, you notice what you missed before. The ink changes.

Red on some pages. Blue on others. Black on the last few.

---

Three pages. Three inks. The same few words on each, written as if for the first time.

Someone met her, and forgot, and met her again. And again.

(A new page is open in the old book.)


=== three_inks_late
when: 11+
requires: dreamed_of_her
blocks: three_inks
picture: inks
sets: three_inks

Turning the old book in your hands, you notice the ink changes from page to page. Red. Blue. Black.

Three pages, written in three inks, all saying the same thing as if for the first time.

(A new page is open in the old book.)


# ------------------------------------------------------------ ACT THREE: THE TRUTH

=== the_hand
after: three_inks 1
picture: chamber
sets: journal_is_mine

Late, alone, you write a note about your people in your own journal. Then, without thinking, you lay it beside the old book.

---
picture: journal

The loops of the letters. The way the lines lean, as if always in a hurry.

The hands match.

---

The old book is yours. You wrote every word of it.

And you forgot. More than once.


=== the_harvest
after: journal_is_mine 1
requires: names_known
picture: voices
sets: harvest_known

"You are remembering," says Memory. "You were not meant to remember. Not this time."

"This time?"

---

Time sighs, a sound like a season ending.

"A god who loves a mortal, and a people who believe in that god. Together they make something none of us can make alone."

---
picture: harvest

"Love that rises. We gather it, and we give it to every people in every world, so that they will love their gods too."

"Without it, mortals would remember what we truly are: distant, silent, often cruel. They would stop believing. And every one of us would fade."

---

"So you let me find her," you say. "You let me love her. And then you take it. You take her. You take my memory. And you start again."

"Yes," says Time.

"It was never a punishment," says Memory. "It is a harvest."


=== her_birth
after: harvest_known 1
journal: 70+
blocks: blood_on_the_river
picture: birth
sets: her_born

At dawn, by a river full of reeds, a girl is born.

She does not cry. She looks up.

---

You have rebuilt her world, piece by piece, without knowing what you were doing. And here she is.

The Voices are watching too.


=== no_birth
after: harvest_known 1
journal: under 70
blocks: her_born, blood_on_the_river
picture: village
sets: no_birth

You search the world for her.

You find rivers, and songs, and good people who are not her.

---

The world you built is real, and alive in its own way. But it is not her world. She is not born here.

Not this time.


=== no_birth_blood
after: harvest_known 1
requires: blood_on_the_river
picture: fog
sets: no_birth

You search the world for her.

You find the river where your people killed a girl in your name. The reeds still grow there. Nothing else does.

---

Whatever world she could have been born into, your people ended it on that riverbank.

She is not born here. Not this time.
