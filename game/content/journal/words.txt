# WHICH WORDS IN THE OLD BOOK BECOME READABLE, AND WHEN
#
# Format:   discovery-or-flag: word, word, word
#
# Words tied to a DISCOVERY are "her world": they count toward how much of
# her world you have rebuilt, which decides whether she can be born.
# Words tied to a FLAG are story words: they unlock when the story gets there.
# Any word NOT listed here becomes readable with First Words (speech).

# --- Her world (discoveries) ---
fire: fire, warm, light
song: sang, songs, singing
river: river, reeds, rain, water
weaving: weaving
sunrise_burial: buried, dead, sunrise, wake
stone: stone, built
temple: temple, roof
stargazing: stars
pottery: clay, bowl

# --- The story (flags) ---
# She is a mystery until the dream (around halfway).
dreamed_of_her: her, she, laughed, hand, shoulder, mother, grandfather
# "I" is a mystery until the god realizes the book is his own.
journal_is_mine: i, me, my, myself
# The deepest truths unlock last.
harvest_known: time, voices, punishment, taken, again, met
