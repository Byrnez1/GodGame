=== page_one
order: 1
title: The first page
sketch: reeds bending in moving water, a small fire, and a figure standing far out among the reeds, its face rubbed away

Her mother sang the old songs by the fire, weaving reeds from the river. I sat where the light could not reach me and listened until the stars went out.

=== page_two
order: 2
title: A page with a pressed flower
sketch: a mound of earth on a hillside, with the sun rising behind it
when: 5+

She laughed at me for watching, and brought me fish in a clay bowl, as if gods were hungry. She told me her people buried their dead facing the sunrise, so they would always wake to something warm. I asked what I would wake to. She did not answer. She only took my hand.

=== page_three
order: 3
title: A water-stained page
sketch: a house of stacked stones, with wavy lines rising from its roof, like singing
when: 7+

We hid from the storm in the stone temple her grandfather built. Rain on the roof like a thousand small hands. She fell asleep against my shoulder, and for the first time since I have existed, I was afraid of time.

=== page_inks
order: 4
title: Three pages, three inks

I met her today. I have never felt anything like this.

I met her today. I have never felt anything like this.

I met her today. I have never felt anything like this, and I do not know why my hand is shaking as I write it.

=== page_last
order: 5
title: The last page
ink: black, pressed hard into the page
requires: journal_is_mine

If I am reading this, they have taken everything again. Find the river. Find the songs. Find her. And when the voices tell me it was a punishment, I must not believe them.
