God Game · the world view, round two

Between decisions

Five scenes you might watch while time passes and events unfold. Each one is drawn live in code, the same way we'd build it in Godot. I gave each a different look and a different kind of event, so you can react to the feel, not just the colors.

Two views

The Pantheon view records your decisions, their effects, and the paths you missed. The World view shows your people living.

Alive, not a card game

People moving, working, reacting. Prayers come from inside the world rather than appearing as cards.

Glimpses of the future

Your idea: as faith grows, the Pantheon view starts showing faint possible outcomes. Noted for the next round.

How it could fit together

The World view plays continuously. When someone prays, the camera eases toward them and their words appear in the scene, as Sana's do in The Flood. Your answers appear as quiet text in the world, with no cards. Different events can use different camera views: side view for village life, a close-up for intimate moments, the map for journeys. Between turns, a time-lapse like Generations shows the years passing. Switching to the Pantheon view shows your past decisions, their consequences, the paths you missed, and, with enough faith, faint glimpses of what might come.
