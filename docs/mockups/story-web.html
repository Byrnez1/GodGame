Where the player has a say today

  • Whether she is born. Decided by how much of her world you rebuilt, which comes from the discoveries you granted. This is the tech tree steering the story.
  • Ina's knife. Tell the people you want a sacrifice, then let the knife fall, and she is never born. The only story fork driven by a choice in a prayer.
  • When the book reveals itself. Taking Time's gift or telling Memory about the small room delays the three inks. The story still arrives, just later.
  • The final choice. Love, fear, or let it happen again, and a cast decides if it holds.
  • Epilogue lines. Oren, Sana's grave, blood on the river, and whether you were loved or feared add a sentence at the very end.

How the proposal works

  • Six eras, 36 generations. About twice as long as today. Eras 2 and 4 have no big turning point: they are where your investments play out.
  • Four turning points where the player commits to a direction: how to treat the Voices, what they believe she is, what to do with the truth, and how to face the end.
  • Your people become something. Love, fear, and silence add up over the eras into a culture with its own prayers, institutions that act on their own, and exiles who come back. It decides which paths come easily and which final options appear.
  • Quiet generations. Some generations pass in a single summary (“Three generations pass. Your temple grows. Fear of you has become law.”). They keep a longer game from dragging, and show investments paying off.
  • Paths, not stats. Each answer sets a path flag. Later cutscenes, prayers, and the Voices' interference check it, so the same story moment plays differently on each path.
  • The finale reflects the path. Which final options appear depends on the paths taken, and the shooting-star cast decides whether the attempt holds.
  • What it costs to build. Paths need nothing new in the engine: flags, requires, and blocks already do it. A culture needs one new idea (love and fear tracked across eras, not just right now). The biggest cost is writing: roughly 80 to 100 prayers instead of 48, plus cutscene versions for each path.

Small choices that should come back

Today, many choices are remembered only as a line in the Known tab. These would pay them off inside the paths above.

  • The woman in the reeds. If your people feared her, the girl is born into suspicion. If they called her an omen, they welcome her.
  • Sana's burial. Sunrise or sky decides where the girl is born and the song she hums.
  • Oren, given or spared. His descendants stand with you, or against you, in the finale.
  • Time's gift. If you took it, your memory of her is incomplete, and one finale option is missing.
  • The strangers. Welcomed or driven off, they decide whose blood the girl carries and who protects her.
  • The truer verse. If you taught it, your people take the truth about the harvest better.
  • Haro and the other altar. A rival faith the Voices can use against you in Act III.
