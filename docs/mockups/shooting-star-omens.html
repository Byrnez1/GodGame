What the interference does
  • Full moon. Its glare hides your star near it, but it hides the other stars too.
  • Memory's fog. Drifting banks hide whatever is behind them. In The Reading, the fog also erases your trail as you draw.
  • Time's skip. Every few seconds part of the sky jumps. In The Turning Sky the whole wheel jumps. In Clear the Sky it happens the moment your star falls.
